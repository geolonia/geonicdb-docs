# Sign Up

GeonicDB SaaS operates on an **invitation-only** model. There is no self-service sign-up. Access is granted through a managed onboarding process with the Geolonia team.

## How to Request Access

1. Visit [https://www.geolonia.com/contact/](https://www.geolonia.com/contact/)
2. Fill in the contact form with the following information:
   - **Company / Organization name**
   - **Use case** — describe what you want to build (e.g., IoT sensor management, smart city data platform, FIWARE migration)
   - **Estimated data volume** — number of entities, update frequency
   - **Region preference** — Japan (ap-northeast-1) or other
3. Submit the form — Geolonia will respond within **1–2 business days**

## What Happens Next

After your request is reviewed, Geolonia will:

1. **Evaluate your use case** — ensure GeonicDB SaaS fits your requirements
2. **Provision your tenant** — create an isolated tenant with the name you choose
3. **Send an invitation email** — with account activation instructions and your initial API key
4. **Schedule an onboarding call** (optional) — walk through setup together

## Access Scope

Each account includes:

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Tenant | One dedicated tenant (additional tenants available on request) |
| API access | Full NGSIv2 and NGSI-LD APIs |
| Console access | app.geonicdb.com (Coming Soon — managed during onboarding) |
| Support | Email support via your Geolonia account manager |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a free trial?**
Contact Geolonia to discuss trial options. We evaluate each request case-by-case.

**Can I add team members to my account?**
Yes. After your account is active, contact your account manager to add additional users.

**What regions are supported?**
GeonicDB SaaS is currently hosted in Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1). Other regions are on the roadmap.

**Can I self-host instead?**
GeonicDB's self-hosted option is currently not publicly available. Contact Geolonia to discuss your deployment requirements.
