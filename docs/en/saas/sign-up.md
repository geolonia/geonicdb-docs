# Sign Up

Step 1 of the SaaS onboarding flow: submit a contact request to Geolonia.

::: tip Invitation-only access
GeonicDB SaaS does **not** have a self-service sign-up page. Access is granted through a managed onboarding process.
:::

## How to Request Access

1. Visit [https://www.geolonia.com/contact/](https://www.geolonia.com/contact/)
2. Fill in the contact form with:
   - **Company / Organization name**
   - **Use case** — describe what you want to build (e.g., IoT sensor management, smart city data platform, FIWARE migration)
   - **Estimated data volume** — number of entities, update frequency
   - **Region preference** — Japan (ap-northeast-1) or other
3. Submit the form — Geolonia will respond within **1–2 business days**

## What Happens Next

After submitting, Geolonia reviews your request and contacts you to discuss approval and tenant provisioning.
